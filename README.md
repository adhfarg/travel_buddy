# Travel Buddy App

Welcome to Travel Buddy!

# Description:

Travel Buddy is an app designed to make your travel experience in the NYC Manhattan area more convenient. Whether you're looking for hotels or restaurants nearby, Travel Buddy has got you covered. With its intuitive interface and powerful features, finding the perfect place to stay or dine has never been easier.

# Key Features:

User Authentication: Create your account with a username and password, or conveniently sign up using your Google or Apple credentials.

Homepage: Upon logging in, you'll be directed to the homepage where you'll find a wealth of information about the NYC Manhattan area, powered by Google's API.

Search Functionality: Easily search for different locations within the NYC area to find the best hotels and restaurants tailored to your preferences.

Detailed Place Information: Click on any listed place to view detailed information, including contact details, address, and opening hours.

Logout Option: When you're done exploring or need to take a break, simply log out of your account with ease.

# How to Use:

Login or Sign Up: Start by logging in with your username and password, or choose to sign up using your Google or Apple account.

Explore the Homepage: Once logged in, explore the homepage to discover oncoming data for the NYC Manhattan area, including hotels and restaurants.

Search for Locations: Use the search functionality to find specific locations within the NYC area.

View Details: Click on any hotel or restaurant to view detailed information about the location, including contact details, address, and opening hours.

Logout: When you're finished using the app, logout from your account to ensure your privacy and security.

# Note:

This app is specifically designed for users seeking information about hotels and restaurants in the NYC Manhattan area.

Google's API powers the data fetching functionality, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information.

For the best experience, make sure you have an active internet connection.
